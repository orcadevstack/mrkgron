## Purpose

Billing records the commercial relationship between an organization and Mrkgron, including plan selection, invoices, and payment-related administration.

## Operating guidance

Use an authorized billing contact. Review invoice details against the agreed commercial terms. Contact the Mrkgron team through [/contact](/contact) for account changes, invoicing questions, or enterprise commercial requirements.

## Security

Do not transmit payment credentials through free-text support messages. Use approved billing or enterprise account channels.
