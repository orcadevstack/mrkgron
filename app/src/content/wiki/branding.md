## Logo

Use the approved Mrkgron wordmark without stretching, recoloring, shadowing, or distortion. The primary wordmark uses black with a restrained #EE6C4D emphasis.

## Color system

- Black: #000000
- White: #FFFFFF
- Accent: #EE6C4D

## Typography and spacing

Use Inter at the approved weights. Use only the shared 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, and 48 pixel spacing scale. Components must use shared visual primitives rather than locally improvised variants.
