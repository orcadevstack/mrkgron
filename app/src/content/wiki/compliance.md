## Purpose

Compliance describes the operating controls used to manage access, data handling, and accountable platform activity.

## Core practices

- Restrict workspace access by role and business need.
- Review audit-relevant events and system changes regularly.
- Retain records according to contractual and regulatory requirements.
- Escalate security or privacy concerns through approved support channels.
