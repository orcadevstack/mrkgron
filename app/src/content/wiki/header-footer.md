## Header system

The global header provides the Mrkgron wordmark, primary navigation, account access, and the primary trial action. It remains fixed at 72 pixels with a white surface and controlled active-route indication.

## Footer system

The footer provides platform links, legal links, copyright, and the approved social links for LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and YouTube. Social controls are intentionally contained in the footer.

## Branding rules

Shared navigation uses Inter, black text, white surfaces, and #EE6C4D for active emphasis. Do not create separate header or footer variants within product modules.
