## Purpose

Mrkgron is the operating record for accountable customer, communications, commerce, and organizational decisions. It combines data, workflow controls, and reporting into one governed workspace.

## Mission

Make operational information clear, controlled, and usable at the point of decision.

## Vision

Establish a consistent operating system for organizations that require durable governance and accountable growth.

## Core capabilities

- Customer relationship management and identity resolution.
- Communications, campaigns, journeys, and delivery operations.
- Analytics, reporting, commerce, and logistics visibility.
- Integrations, tenant controls, and workspace governance.
