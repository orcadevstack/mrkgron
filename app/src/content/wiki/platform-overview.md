## Operating model

Mrkgron combines customer, communications, commerce, and operating data into a controlled workspace. Each module records a distinct operating responsibility while sharing workspace identity and access controls.

## Core modules

- [Dashboard](/wiki/dashboard) provides executive performance and priority visibility.
- [Accounts](/wiki/accounts) controls workspace user access.
- [Transactions](/wiki/transactions) records commercial activity.
- [Investments](/wiki/investments) provides investment-oriented reporting context.
- [Compliance](/wiki/compliance) documents governance and audit practices.
- [Billing](/wiki/billing) records commercial service controls.
- [Settings](/wiki/settings) manages workspace preferences.

## Related operational modules

CRM manages customer records. Communications manages campaigns. Analytics records reporting and events. Journeys and automation coordinate repeatable activity. Integrations connect approved external services.
