## Purpose

Premium filters refine operational datasets without reloading the page. They provide controlled, predictable selection for status, category, and other supported record attributes.

## Use filters

1. Select the relevant filter value.
2. Review the updated results and empty state.
3. Change or clear the selection to return to the broader record set.

## Performance and accessibility

Mrkgron filter controls are controlled components with typed values and derived results. Search-oriented filters use debounced values where appropriate. Keyboard focus and active selection are visible through the institutional accent treatment.
